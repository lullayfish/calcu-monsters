CALCU-MONSTERS — Calculator Prototype
=====================================
Open project.godot in Godot 4.7 and press F6/F5 to run.

This is the calculator foundation ONLY; monsters and timers are intentionally not implemented yet.

Click the illustrated calculator buttons, or use keyboard digits, + - * /, Enter, Backspace, and Escape.
The art is taken from your uploaded Calculator-ASSETS.zip (calculator_example_02.png).

Controls: C clears; DEL deletes; +/- changes sign; % divides by 100; . enters decimals.
Arithmetic follows standard precedence (multiplication/division before addition/subtraction).
